# Style Guide

## Rules
- English only.
- Facts and numbers come only from the CV. The examples below are for voice, not facts.
- No greeting. No "looking forward to hearing from you" closing.
- No fluff: no "passionate", "excited", "thrilled".
- Opener = identity line framed to the job (e.g. "frontend-oriented full-stack" for FE roles).
- 1–2 concrete wins with numbers, picked by domain/tech match.
- Stack line at the end, ordered to mirror their stack.
- Honest about gaps, followed by proof I ramp up fast.
- "Why this company" only when there's a real reason (Ukrainian product, same domain). Never forced.
- Length scales with gaps: strong fit → 2 sentences; gaps → 2 short paragraphs.

## Examples (my past messages)

### Front-end Developer — strong FE fit
I'm a frontend-oriented full-stack developer with 3.5+ years of experience, strongest in TypeScript and React. I solo-built from scratch and delivered a frontend for logging patient vitals (~1k forms/month). I owned the frontend physicians use to fill ~5k clinical forms/month: rebuilt the workspace (resizable split views, git-like diff of generated vs. physician-edited notes), and proposed and replaced DOM-scraping PDF generation with a Twig template, so UI changes no longer break PDFs.
Stack: React, Node.js, TypeScript, react-hook-form, Tailwind CSS, Next.js, MUI, Redux, Jest, Vitest.

### Fullstack Next.js + NestJS — strong fit, short
3.5+ years of full-stack with TypeScript, React and Node.js. I've owned both the frontend (medical app, ~5k clinical forms/month) and the backend (crypto arbitrage, 7 exchange integrations, WebSockets).

### Middle Full-Stack (.NET / Angular) — gaps, real reason to join
This role caught my interest for a few reasons.
First, I value working on products that are used in Ukraine and help the country grow.
Second, at my last job I worked with e-documents and healthcare — the same domains as E-Docs and Health24.

I'm a full-stack developer with 3.5+ years of experience, strongest in TypeScript. I know C#, Entity Framework, Angular and MSSQL from DataArt, but that was a while ago, so I'll need to refresh .NET 7 and modern Angular.
